# Ilustraciones de los bloques

Las tres ilustraciones de `EfectoDomino/Assets.xcassets/ilustracion-*` (gimnasio, caminata y en casa) se generaron el sábado 24 de octubre con ChatGPT Images, encargadas a Codex CLI desde Claude Code. Primero se generó el gimnasio; las otras dos usaron esa imagen como referencia de estilo y de personaje. Después se recortaron a una banda de 1536 × 640 y se bajaron a 1200 × 500 en JPEG.

Aparecen arriba de las hojas de un bloque (Ajustar, Registrar y el detalle), como las de los eventos de Google Calendar. Con texto de accesibilidad se ocultan.

## Dirección de arte (igual para las tres)

```
ART DIRECTION (same for every illustration in this set):
Flat vector editorial illustration in the spirit of Google Calendar's event header illustrations (the banners that appear on events like gym, tennis or cooking): a wide landscape scene, simple geometric shapes, gentle rounded forms, calm and friendly, lots of negative space.
Palette strictly limited and whitish: warm off-white and cream backgrounds (#FBF6EF, #F4EADD), soft oranges and peaches (#F28A55, #F7B48C, #FBD9C3) with the strong orange #EE6121 used sparingly as the accent, pure white (#FFFFFF) for thin line details and highlights, and at most a touch of warm light grey (#E6DED3). No other hues: no blue, no green, no purple, no black, no dark outlines.
Rendering: flat fills, thin white line work, no heavy gradients, no 3D shadows, no texture noise.
Character: Camila, a woman in her mid-30s with dark brown hair tied back, medium size in the scene, faceless or with minimal features, simple sportswear in cream and orange tones.
Composition: 1536x1024 landscape. Keep the subject and all important shapes inside the central horizontal band so the image still reads when cropped to a 3:1 strip; leave the left third calmer.
Never include text, letters, numbers, logos, UI, borders or frames.
```

## Escenas

- **Gimnasio:** Camila at the gym, lifting a pair of light dumbbells next to a weight bench, a small rack with a few dumbbells, and a large window with soft light shapes behind her.
- **Caminata:** Camila walking briskly outdoors on a park path, rounded trees and soft rolling hills behind her (evoking the hills around Santiago de Chile), a pale sun as a simple circle, a bench far to one side.
- **En casa:** Camila at home doing a gentle stretching and mobility pose on a yoga mat in her living room, a potted plant, a low shelf with a couple of books, and a window with warm evening light.
